Spirit of Resilience

Artist: Rita Alorti

The spirit of resilience among Africans as they fight for total freedom and independence amidst threats, fear and manipulation.

Spirit of Resilience honours the determination of Africans as they stand for total freedom and independence in the face of threats, fear and manipulation. Printed in black, deep red and grey on a clean white ground, the cloth sets bold portraits against repeating geometric motifs, proud deer heads and a single flower.

Every repeat carries the same message: pressure may come, but the spirit holds.
