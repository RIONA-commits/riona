Featured textile

Kluvinyenye-Ŋkɔ bubu si wozi ɖe ame dzi

Artist: Rita Alorti

Woven for freedom. Printed for pride. Worn for liberation.

This cloth was made with Africa’s liberation in mind. Its patterns carry resilience, unity and independence, so that what is worn says what it stands for.

Printed in black, deep red and grey on a white ground, this cloth sets three bold portraits among repeating geometric motifs, proud deer heads and a small flower. The riona mark sits at its centre.

The artist’s statement

The piece portrays three prominent political leaders from different geographical areas. On the left, Donald Trump, and on the right, Emmanuel Macron, represent the roots of colonization in Africa. They appear to project supremacy and strength over Ibrahim Traoré, in the middle, who represents Africa and calls for African unity to fight for total independence and liberation. It carries the spirit of resilience among Africans as they fight for total freedom and independence amidst threats, fear and manipulation.

— Rita Alorti
