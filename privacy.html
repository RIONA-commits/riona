Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

riona (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what happens to information when you use this website.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no tracking tools and no advertising cookies. We do not run a server that stores your personal details.

What stays on your device

  • Theme choice: your light or dark mode choice.
  • Order basket: the items you add to your order.

Both are saved only in your own browser and never sent to us. You can remove them any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

When you contact us

  • Feedback: the form opens your email app with your message ready. We only receive it if you press send in your email app.
  • Orders: the order button opens WhatsApp with your order ready. We only receive it if you send it, and WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to that message.

We use what you send us only to reply to you, prepare your order and improve our work. We do not sell your information.

Other services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests the page. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

This website is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You may ask us what messages we hold from you, or ask us to delete them, by contacting us using the WhatsApp and Email buttons on the Feedback page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
